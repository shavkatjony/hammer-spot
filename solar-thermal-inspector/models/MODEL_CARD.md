# Model card: `cnn-dbb19326af`

Generated by `training/evaluate.py`. Numbers are from the held-out test split unless stated.

## Intended use

Triage of drone infrared images of **single PV module crops**: flag modules that need attention, suggest the fault type and a priority, and route uncertain cases to a person. A technician confirms every work order on site. It is not meant for unattended shutdown decisions, for safety certification, or for image types it was not trained on (full frames, visible-light images, other equipment).

## Model

- Small CNN, 288,428 parameters (width 32), input 1 x 40 x 24 grey levels / 255, 12 softmax outputs.
- Trained 40 epochs (best epoch 40 by validation macro-F1), AdamW + OneCycle (max LR 0.003), batch 128, label smoothing 0.05, square-root inverse-frequency class weights; augmentation: horizontal/vertical flips, Gaussian noise (sd 2 grey levels).
- Flip test-time augmentation: yes. Temperature scaling T = 0.7 (validation ECE 0.124 -> 0.012).
- Exported to ONNX (opset 17), served with ONNX Runtime on CPU, SHA-256 `dbb19326afdceabc...`.

## Decision rule

- Alert when P(fault) = 1 - P(No-Anomaly) >= **0.382** (validation recall 95%).
- `ALERT_POLICY=safety` also alerts when P(critical classes) >= **0.063** (validation critical recall 97%). Critical: Diode, Diode-Multi, Hot-Spot-Multi, Offline-Module.
- Alerts with top-class probability < 0.5 are marked *needs review*.

## Data

InfraredSolarModules (Raptor Maps, MIT): 19,972 images after removing exact duplicates and 6 groups of identical images with conflicting labels; split 70/15/15 stratified by class, seed 42 (`data/splits.csv`).

## Known limitations

- Offline-Module recall is 0.63: a uniformly warm module is hard to tell from a normal one without its neighbours. Use the safety policy or the *level vs batch* value.
- Soiling recall is 0.23 on 30 test images; rare classes have wide uncertainty.
- Strong sensor noise degrades recall (see robustness table). Global brightness offsets do not.
- Trained on one provider's 8-bit crops with no site IDs; validate and fine-tune on a new camera or site before relying on it.
